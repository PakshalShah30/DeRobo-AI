    Less robotic.

    01

    Find the tells

    Stock openers, "it is important to note that", stacked "Furthermore"s, buzzwords and sentences that all run the same length.

    02

    Fix, don't invent

    Rewrites remove padding and swap jargon for plain words. Expand adds prompts for your details instead of making claims up.

    Stay in control

    Pick the strength and style, lock your own terms, and see every change highlighted before you copy it.
